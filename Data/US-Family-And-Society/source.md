### Married population aged 15 and older (`adultsMarried`)

- **Publisher:** computed from the U.S. Census Bureau historical family and living-arrangement tables (counts divided within the same table)
- **Where:** <https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/demo/tables/families/time-series/marital/ms1.xls>
- **Unit:** percent
- **Coverage:** 1950–2025
- **Note:** Computed from married men plus married women divided by total men plus total women, all races, aged 15 and older. Includes spouse absent and separated. Same-sex married couples included since 2019.
- **Breaks:** 2014 ASEC questionnaire redesign; decennial population-control revisions (the publisher's lettered r rows, taken over the original); 2021 appears twice in the tables (the later-method row is taken); 2025 uses Vintage-2025 population controls; 2019 onward includes same-sex married couples.

- **Where:** <https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/popest/tables/2020-2025/national/asrh/nc-est2025-agesex.xlsx> · <https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/popest/tables/2000-2010/intercensal/national/us-est00int-01.xls> · <https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/popest/tables/2010-2020/intercensal/national/asrh/nc-est2020int-agesex.xlsx>
- **Unit:** %
- **Coverage:** 2000–2025
- **Note:** Computed from all-sexes age-65-and-older counts divided by all-sexes total resident population. Vintage list: 2000–2010 intercensal estimates for 2000–2009; 2010–2020 intercensal estimates for 2010–2019; Vintage 2025 estimates for 2020–2025. Newer vintage wins at 2010 and 2020. A22 compares April 1 census/base observations at the same dates, allowing 0.3 years or 0.2 percentage points. The 1970–1999 extension is deferred: the historical Census workbooks cover resident population plus armed forces overseas, or single-year ages without a published median, so they are not joined (evidence in work/population-report.md).
- **Breaks:** Intercensal/vintage splice at 2010 and 2020; census-base revisions can change estimates. July annual estimates are not replaced by April census/base observations.

### Adults aged 25–34 living with parents (`youngAdultsWithParents`)

- **Publisher:** computed from the U.S. Census Bureau historical family and living-arrangement tables (counts divided within the same table)
- **Where:** <https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/demo/tables/families/time-series/adults/ad1.xls>
- **Unit:** percent
- **Coverage:** 1960–2025
- **Note:** Computed from male plus female children of householders divided by male plus female population aged 25–34, never averaging sex-specific percentages. Unmarried college students in dormitories are counted in their parents' homes in CPS data.
- **Breaks:** 2014 ASEC questionnaire redesign; decennial population-control revisions (the publisher's lettered r rows, taken over the original); 2021 appears twice in the tables (the later-method row is taken); 2025 uses Vintage-2025 population controls; 2019 onward includes same-sex married couples.
