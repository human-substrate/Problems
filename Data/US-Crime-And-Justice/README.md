# US Crime and Justice

23 annual U.S. series shipped; 0 requested keys deferred. Generated by docs.ts from index.json and series JSON; never edit this file directly.

Values come from sealed cached primary sources. No network, interpolation, or smoothing. Gaps are retained. Computed series state their formulas; fiscal years use the fiscal-year number. Do not equate beliefs, police reporting, survey victimization, correctional response, and voluntary online complaints. Each series carries its own unit, breaks, method, bounds, and source URLs. A named 12-point exception applies to jailRate; all other shipped series have at least 15 observations.

## Reproduce

```sh
bun --env-file=$HOME/.claude/.env update.ts
bun test lib/checks.test.ts
bun docs.ts
bun update.ts --only ncvs,gss
```

Bun and the existing .cache snapshot are required; there are zero npm dependencies. update.ts verifies SHA-256 hashes against sources.json before parsing. It refuses changed or missing inputs and continues independent groups, exiting nonzero for unexpected errors. --only accepts group names or individual keys and merges the index. Groups: audit, fbi, ncvs, corrections, capital, federal, gallup, gss, ftc, ic3. update.log is appended on each build, including deferred-key evidence. FBI CDE rows use FBI_API_KEY when present, else DEMO_KEY (rate-limited; a 429 defers with the live body).

## Files

- index.json: catalog with coverage, point counts, latest values, and metadata.
- series/<key>.json: {_meta, data}, ascending four-digit year keys.
- sources.json: raw-cache URLs, read date, and immutable SHA-256 fingerprints. Preserve .cache for offline replay.
- deferred.json: evidenced deferrals (empty when every key ships).
- lib/checks.test.ts: 19 tests pinned to cached publisher values or independently computed GSS shares.
- source.md: detailed provenance and methodology.
- work/recovery.md and work/mismatches.md: recovery evidence, rejected stitches, and sample differences.
