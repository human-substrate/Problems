# Sources — US Employment & Jobs in the AI era

The per-series provenance (publisher, exact endpoint or document, cadence, annual rule, and method note) is generated from the data and lives in [`Data/US-Employment-And-Jobs/source.md`](../../Data/US-Employment-And-Jobs/source.md). This file lists the publishers by class and the study sources quoted in the README.

## Primary statistical publishers (outcome series)

- **Bureau of Labor Statistics** — Current Employment Statistics (sector employment, via FRED); Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey (openings, hires, layoffs & discharges, via FRED); Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (national workbooks `oesm19nat.zip` … `oesm25nat.zip`, read by SOC code).
- **US Employment and Training Administration** — weekly initial unemployment-insurance claims (via FRED ICSA).
- **Federal Reserve Bank of New York** — The Labor Market for Recent College Graduates (CSV files behind the interactive: unemployment and underemployment, ages 22–27 with a bachelor's degree, monthly SA 3-month average since 1990).
- **Indeed Hiring Lab** — Job Postings Index (total, Software Development, Customer Service; published on FRED and on GitHub under CC BY 4.0).

## Publishers of the phenomenon series (AI itself)

- **US Census Bureau** — Business Trends and Outlook Survey, AI questions (two workbooks, two wordings, biweekly since September 2023).
- **Real-Time Population Survey** (Bick, Blandin & Deming), hosted by the St. Louis Fed on FRED — generative-AI adoption for work, quarterly since August 2024.
- **Gallup** — workforce study AI-use items (annual 2023–2024, quarterly from Q2 2025); Bentley–Gallup Business in Society study (annual since 2023).
- **Indeed Hiring Lab** — AI Tracker (share of postings mentioning AI, daily since January 2019, GitHub).
- **Challenger, Gray & Christmas** — Job Cut Announcement Report, job cuts by reason (AI as a reason since 2023).
- **Ramp Economics Lab** — Ramp AI Index (monthly since January 2023).
